**Working Title:** *Slow Is Not the Same as Safe: Engineering Leadership Inside Regulated Industries*

**One-Line Pitch:** A CTO who has spent seventeen years building lending infrastructure inside heavy regulation makes the case that most "safety" processes in regulated engineering orgs aren't protecting anything anymore - and shows leaders how to tell the difference between real risk controls and inherited habit.

**Overview:**
Every engineering leader in a regulated industry - fintech, healthcare, insurance, banking infrastructure - inherits processes built to prevent a specific failure at a specific point in the company's history. Change-approval boards, multi-week compliance reviews, mandatory sign-off chains. Over time, the original failure gets engineered away, but the process protecting against it never gets retired - it just becomes "how we do things here," and it quietly taxes velocity for no corresponding safety benefit. Drawing on her three years as CTO of Ledgerly and fourteen years building payments and lending systems before that, Priya Chandrasekaran argues that the real job of an engineering leader in a regulated company isn't choosing between speed and safety - it's constantly re-auditing which of your processes are still buying you safety, and killing the ones that are just buying you comfort. The book combines her own team's stories - including a nine-day approval delay on a bug that was actively overcharging customers - with a practical framework any engineering leader can use to distinguish genuine risk controls from inherited ritual, and to rebuild trust with regulators and engineers at the same time.

**Sample Chapter Titles:**

1. **"The Fourteen-Person Room"** - The story of inheriting a change-approval board that had outlived the failure it was built to prevent, and the nine-day delay that forced Priya to finally question it.
2. **"Where the Rework Hides"** - Why velocity problems in regulated industries are almost always trust or information problems in disguise, using the compliance-review case study from Ledgerly's product engineering team.
3. **"Auditable Isn't the Enemy of Fast"** - A practical framework for building audit trails, override paths, and regulator-facing documentation into design from day one, so compliance stops being a late-stage tax on shipped work.
