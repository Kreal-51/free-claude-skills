# Filled Accounting Treatment Memo — IFRS 16 Lease Example

```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ACCOUNTING TREATMENT — New Office Lease, 5-Year Term
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

APPLICABLE STANDARD
IFRS 16 Leases, paragraphs 22–28 (initial recognition and measurement)

TREATMENT SUMMARY
5-year lease for Floor 3, Riverside House, commencing 01/04/2026. Monthly
rent £8,500, no purchase option, no renewal reasonably certain. Recognise
a right-of-use (ROU) asset and lease liability at present value of remaining
payments, discounted at incremental borrowing rate of 6.5%.

JOURNAL ENTRIES
Initial recognition (01/04/2026):
  Dr  Right-of-Use Asset (Non-current 1610)     £432,900
  Cr  Lease Liability (Non-current 2610)                  £432,900
  Being: Initial recognition of 5-year office lease, PV of 60 monthly
  payments of £8,500 discounted at 6.5% IBR, per IFRS 16 lease schedule
  built in supporting workbook "Riverside_Lease_Schedule_2026.xlsx"

Subsequent measurement (monthly, e.g. April 2026):
  Dr  Interest Expense (P&L 7310)                  £2,345
  Dr  Lease Liability (2610)                       £6,155
  Cr  Cash / Bank (1010)                                    £8,500
  Being: April lease payment split between interest (6.5% on opening
  liability) and principal reduction, per amortisation schedule

  Dr  Depreciation Expense (P&L 7210)               £7,215
  Cr  Accumulated Depreciation — ROU Asset (1615)             £7,215
  Being: Straight-line depreciation of ROU asset over 60-month lease term
  (£432,900 / 60 months)

DISCLOSURE REQUIREMENTS
Note disclosure of: ROU asset by class, lease liability maturity analysis,
total cash outflow for leases, discount rate applied, and a description of
the leasing arrangement (IFRS 16.51–53).

KEY JUDGMENTS
Incremental borrowing rate of 6.5% determined by reference to company's
existing secured borrowing rate, adjusted for the 5-year term. No renewal
option judged reasonably certain — landlord confirmed no automatic renewal
clause; excluded from lease term.

DOCUMENTATION REQUIRED
Signed lease agreement, IBR derivation memo, lease schedule workbook showing
full amortisation, and board approval minute for the new office space.

ALTERNATIVE TREATMENTS
Low-value/short-term lease exemptions not applicable (term exceeds 12
months and asset is not low-value).
```
