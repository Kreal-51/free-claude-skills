# Q3 2026 Board Meeting Pack - Executive Summary

*Illustrative worked example. Northline Robotics and every customer, investor and
figure below are invented; they are here to show the format, the arithmetic and the
level of detail, not to report a real company.*

**Company:** Northline Robotics, Inc. (Series C, warehouse automation)
**Meeting date:** October 22, 2026
**Prepared for:** Board of Directors
**Prepared by:** Office of the CEO

## Headline

Northline closed Q3 at $4.1M net new ARR (108% of plan), but cash runway
tightened to 14 months on the back of accelerated hiring in Field Ops. The
board needs to approve a $2.5M bridge extension this meeting to preserve
optionality ahead of the Series D conversation in Q1 2027. Everything else in
this pack is context for that one decision.

## The Numbers

| Metric | Q3 Actual | Q3 Plan | Q2 Actual |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARR | $20.0M | $19.7M | $15.9M |
| Net new ARR | $4.1M | $3.8M | $3.2M |
| Gross margin | 71% | 73% | 69% |
| Net revenue retention | 118% | 115% | 121% |
| Cash balance | $9.8M | $10.1M | $11.9M |
| Monthly burn | $700K | $610K | $540K |
| Runway | 14 months | 16 months | 22 months |

Revenue is ahead of plan. Burn is ahead of plan too - Field Ops headcount grew
from 22 to 34 to support the Brightway Markets and Harveston Grocery distribution-center rollouts,
and that growth was not fully reflected in the Q3 budget the board approved in
June.

## Three Things the Board Should Take Away

1. **Growth is real and durable.** The Harveston Grocery pilot (3 sites) converted to a
   19-site, 3-year contract worth $6.2M in TCV, signed September 30. This is
   the single largest contract in company history and validates the
   multi-site expansion motion the go-to-market team has been building
   toward since Q1.
2. **Burn got ahead of the plan for a good reason, but it needs a ceiling.**
   Field Ops headcount will hold flat at 34 through Q1 2027. No further
   headcount growth in that function is planned until the Series D closes.
3. **The runway math forces a near-term decision.** At current burn, cash
   crosses the $2M minimum operating threshold in month 11 (September 2027).
   Series D diligence realistically starts in January and takes 10-14 weeks
   to close. The gap between "cash gets uncomfortable" and "Series D closes"
   is too tight without a bridge.

## The Decision Being Asked of the Board

Approve a $2.5M convertible note bridge from existing investors (Ashford
Ventures and Beacon Hill Partners have both verbally indicated appetite for
$1.25M each), priced at a 20% discount to the eventual Series D with a $60M
valuation cap. At $700K a month that takes cash from $9.8M to $12.3M and extends runway from 14
to 17 months, which puts two full quarters of cushion around the Series D raise.

**Alternative considered and rejected:** slowing Field Ops hiring further to
stretch existing cash. Rejected because the Harveston Grocery contract requires the
current headcount to meet the installation schedule in the signed agreement;
missing installation milestones triggers a service-credit penalty clause.

## Agenda Items Requiring a Vote

1. Approval of the $2.5M bridge note (see Term Sheet, Appendix C)

3. Ratification of the Harveston Grocery master services agreement (already
   countersigned under existing signing authority; board ratification is
   procedural)
